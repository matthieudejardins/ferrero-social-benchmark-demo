Data Deletion Instructions

Ferrero France · Social Benchmark (NextUser Marketing App)

What data the app handles

The app reads public metrics of Instagram professional accounts (followers, number of posts, likes and comments counts of recent posts) through the Instagram Business Discovery API, and the list of Facebook Pages you manage so you can choose a source account. It runs entirely in your browser. The app does not store any of your data on a server: your access token is kept only in your browser tab's session storage and is erased when you close the tab.

How to remove the app's access and your data

  1. Close the browser tab of the app: this erases the access token kept in session storage.
  2. On Facebook, go to Settings & privacy > Settings > Apps and websites.
  3. Find NextUser Marketing App and click Remove. This revokes every permission granted to the app.

Questions or deletion requests

As the app keeps no copy of your data, there is nothing else to delete. For any question, contact NextUser through the contact page of nextuser.com.
