Ferrero France · Social Benchmark

Organic benchmark of Ferrero France brand accounts (Nutella, Kinder, Ferrero Rocher, Tic Tac…) against the public Instagram accounts of their direct competitors. Built by NextUser, Ferrero France's social media agency.

Internal use by the Ferrero France social media team and its agency. We read public metrics (followers, posts, likes and comments counts) of brand professional accounts through the Business Discovery API. Nothing is published, resold, or used for advertising targeting. No personal account and no user comment text is accessed.

Step 1 · Log in with Facebook
Permissions requested: instagram_basic, instagram_manage_insights, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, business_management.
Step 2 · Choose the Ferrero brand Page and its Instagram professional account
Uses pages_show_list and pages_read_engagement to list your Pages and their linked Instagram account.
Step 3 · Compare with a competitor brand account
Uses instagram_basic and instagram_manage_insights (Business Discovery).
